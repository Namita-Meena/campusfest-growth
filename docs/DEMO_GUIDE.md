# Recruiter demo guide (about 5 minutes)

The sidebar shows **Simulated campaign week 4**. If someone has changed the data, open **Demo workspace → tick the box → Reset demo workspace**.

1. **Growth Overview.** Forecast vs the 2,600 base and 3,400 stretch targets, alerts, weekly pace, budget.
2. **Campaign Intelligence.** Club Contingent League is selected: 260 registrations by week 4, recommendation **Scale**.
3. In **Update results to date**, change *Completed registrations* from 260 to **140** and press **Save results**. Confirmed registrations are scaled down automatically.
4. The recommendation is now **Stop**. On **Growth Overview** the forecast has dropped (about 2,686 → 2,185).
5. **AI Growth Copilot.** Ask: *Why is Club Contingent League underperforming?* The answer should cite 140 registrations, week 4 and the 150 threshold.
6. Ask: *What corrective action should we take?* then *How much money can we actually reallocate?* Expected: about ₹38,800 of uncommitted club money (below the ₹40,000 cap) and ₹20,000 uncommitted reserve.
7. Ask: *Create a task for this.* A draft appears with **Approve** and **Reject**. Nothing is saved until Approve.
8. Approve it, open **Task Manager**: the task is there with source *AI-generated*. Refresh the browser: the edit and the task are still there.
9. Ask: *What is the average age of festival attendees?* Expected: the data cannot answer (no age field).
10. **Experiments and Budget.** Approve the Stop recommendation: ₹38,800 moves from Club League to Squad Pass, the total stays ₹3,00,000, and approving again is refused. Move the week slider to **8** to see the deposit test reach significance and the guest-pass pilot result.

Other things to try: change deposit-test counts and watch the z-test; set *fraud confirmed* to 1 for ambassadors; ask the copilot *As of 10 March 2025, how many registrations were confirmed?* (1,159) or *How many check-ins were denied?* (50).
